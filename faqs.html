Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about Kaam-Connect? Find answers to some common questions below.

What is Kaam-Connect?

Kaam-Connect is a platform designed to connect customers with local service professionals for everyday household needs.

What services can I find on Kaam-Connect?

You can explore plumbing, electrical services, carpentry, painting, mechanical services and other general household services.

How can I book a service?

Visit our Book a Service page, fill in your details, select the required service and submit your request.

Can service professionals register on Kaam-Connect?

Yes, service professionals can register themselves to offer their services through our platform.

Is Kaam-Connect free to use?

Kaam-Connect is currently an academic project prototype. No actual charges are involved in using the demonstration.

Does Kaam-Connect provide emergency services?

The current prototype does not provide emergency service support.

How can I contact Kaam-Connect?

You can use the contact information provided on our website to reach our project team.
